Common questions

Does the person I meet need the app?

No. Your QR code opens your card in their browser; they can save your details (photo included) straight to their phone contacts.

How do I change my card?

My Card → Edit. Changes are live immediately — anyone opening your link sees the latest version.

How do I scan paper business cards?

Scan tab → point the camera at the card → review the details → Save. The contact lands in your Contacts tab with the source tagged.

What does Pro add?

Unlimited cards and contacts, full design control, card analytics, bulk scanning and more. See Plans & features inside the app (Settings).

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Privacy & compliance → Delete account. This removes your account and all data immediately. You can export everything first from the same screen.
