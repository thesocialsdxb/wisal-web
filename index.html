HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. No paper.

1

Create your card

Photo, logo, role, links — with themes, fonts and accent colours. Separate personas for work, personal and speaking, switched from one screen.

2

Share with a QR

Your branded QR and link are always ready. Anyone can scan it — your card opens in their browser instantly. They install nothing.

3

They save you — you keep them

They save your details to their phone, photo included, and can send theirs back with one tap. Every exchange lands in your contacts, organised.
